About the workshop

Large language models are changing how people search for, compare, and buy products online, and how sellers and platforms run their businesses. Product search, recommendation, advertising, conversational shopping, customer service, seller tools, and business analytics are all being rebuilt around LLMs.

E-commerce is also a hard research setting. Its data is large, multimodal, multilingual, and changes daily, and it ties directly to real user preferences and real transactions.

This is the third edition of LLM4ECommerce, after The Web Conference 2025 and KDD 2025. This year we put more weight on agentic and multi-agent e-commerce: shopping agents, personalized assistants, tool-using agents, and coordination among customers, sellers, platforms, and the AI agents acting for them. We bring together researchers and practitioners to discuss methods, applications, evaluation, safety, and deployment of LLM-powered e-commerce systems.

Call for papers

We welcome papers on methods, datasets, applications, and systems for LLMs in e-commerce. Topics include, but are not limited to:

Foundations

  • Pre-training, fine-tuning, and alignment for e-commerce
  • Prompting, in-context learning, and retrieval-augmented generation
  • Multimodal and personalized LLMs
  • Efficient and continual learning

Agents and multi-agent systems

Special focus this year

  • Shopping and conversational agents
  • Tool-using and reasoning agents
  • Multi-agent interaction and coordination
  • Agent memory, planning, and personalization
  • Evaluation and safety of e-commerce agents

Applications

  • Product search and recommendation
  • Product understanding, generation, and question answering
  • Advertising, marketing, and customer service
  • Seller assistance and business analytics
  • Customer behavior and personalization
  • Datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation

Trustworthy and scalable systems

  • Fairness, privacy, safety, and robustness
  • Hallucination, factuality, and grounding
  • Scalable training, serving, and deployment
  • Efficient, production-ready LLM systems

Submission

Submit papers in English, as a PDF, in the current ACM two-column conference format. For LaTeX, use \documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}. Review is double-blind, so remove author names and affiliations.

  • Long papers Original work, up to 8 pages plus unlimited references and appendix.
  • Short papers Work in progress with preliminary results, or position papers, up to 4 pages plus unlimited references and appendix.

Accepted papers will be presented as posters, and selected papers will also be given a 10-minute oral slot. Following WSDM policy, at least one author of each accepted paper must present in person. Remote presentations are not supported.

Accepted papers will appear in the WSDM 2027 Companion Proceedings.
